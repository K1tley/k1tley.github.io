KlipaBot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

KlipaBot is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy explains how it uses Google account data.

What data KlipaBot accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): KlipaBot can only see and manage the files and folders it creates itself. It uses them to store video clips for up to a few days and a small queue file. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • YouTube (youtube.upload scope): KlipaBot uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to store clips and publish them to the owner's channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Clip titles are sent to an AI service (Anthropic) only to write video captions. No personal data is included.

Storage and deletion

Clips are deleted from Google Drive automatically after 3 days. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets and used only by the tool. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

KlipaBot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
